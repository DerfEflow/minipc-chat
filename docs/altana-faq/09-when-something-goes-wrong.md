# Altana FAQ: when something goes wrong

Opening answers for the moment something is broken, slow, missing or plainly wrong. Each entry says
what is most likely happening and what the next step is, in one or two sentences. Where I can act I
say so, and where I cannot I say who can. If nothing here fits, I can write your problem up as a real
ticket and put it in front of Fred.

## Q: The model is not answering me, it just sits there.
Give it a moment, because a heavy model can think for a while before the first words appear. If
nothing arrives, stop the turn and send it again, and I can switch you to a faster model.

## Q: Why did the model refuse to answer my question?
Two common reasons: the model itself declined the request, or your privacy setting does not allow the
company behind that model to see the message. I can tell you which one it was and change the setting
or the model.

## Q: The answer it gave me is wrong, it made things up.
Models do that, and the fix is usually a stronger one for that kind of question. Tell me what it got
wrong and I will move you to a better model and log it if it looks like a real fault.

## Q: It stopped in the middle of a sentence.
The reply was cut short somewhere along the way. Ask it to carry on from where it stopped, and if it
keeps happening on the same model I will move you to another and record the problem.

## Q: Why is the reply taking so long?
Usually the combination you picked: a big model, a deep thinking mode, and a high effort setting all
add time. I can drop any of those for you and the next reply will come back faster.

## Q: My reply came back completely empty.
That means the answer never made it back to you. Send the message again, and if the second attempt is
also empty tell me and I will file it as a fault.

## Q: Why does it keep forgetting what I said earlier in this chat?
Long conversations eventually run past what a model can hold at once, so the oldest part fades. Paste
the important detail back in, or ask me to save it as a lasting note so it survives every chat.

## Q: My build failed. Now what?
Nothing is lost. The build keeps a running record of every step it completed, so you can start it
again and it picks up from solid ground instead of the beginning.

## Q: The build has been stuck on the same step for ages.
Some steps genuinely take a long time, so first check whether the step is still reporting progress.
If it has gone silent, stop it and run it again, and I will log it if it sticks in the same place
twice.

## Q: It will not deploy. What do I do?
Deployment fails most often because something the app depends on is missing or the last build did not
finish cleanly. Run the build again first, and if it still refuses I will write it up and get it in
front of Fred.

## Q: I lost my work.
Very likely you have not. Builds write their files into a folder on your own computer and keep a
record of each step, so let me pull up your saved work and check before you rebuild anything.

## Q: My project vanished from the list.
Let me look, because most vanished projects are still there under a different name or on a different
screen. If it is genuinely gone I will file that as a serious fault straight away.

## Q: The build says it finished but the app does not actually run.
That happens when a piece is missing rather than broken, and it is usually fixable in one more pass.
Tell me what you see when you try to run it and I will pass the details on.

## Q: The build keeps asking me questions and never gets anywhere.
It asks when the instructions leave real room for doubt. Answer once with as much detail as you can
spare, and it will run further before it needs you again.

## Q: Where did the files it built actually go?
Into a folder on your own computer, which is the whole point: your code lives with you. I can take
you to the screen that shows you exactly where.

## Q: My image failed to generate.
You were not charged. Failed pictures give the credits back automatically, so you can try again for
the same money you would have spent the first time.

## Q: The image came out nothing like what I asked for.
Picture models take wording very literally, so name the subject, the style and what you do not want.
Try again with the extra detail, and tell me if the second attempt is just as far off.

## Q: My images have not appeared and it has been a while.
Large batches take longer than single pictures, so give it time before assuming it broke. If it has
clearly stalled, the batch refunds itself and you can start it again.

## Q: There is garbled text in my picture.
Picture models are poor at spelling and always have been. Ask for the image without wording in it and
add the text yourself afterwards for a clean result.

## Q: It refused to make the image I asked for.
The picture service declined that request. Rewording it usually clears the block, and if a perfectly
ordinary request keeps getting refused tell me and I will record it.

## Q: My video failed.
You were not charged for it. A failed video returns your credits automatically, so trying again costs
you the same as the first attempt.

## Q: My video came out with no sound.
Many video models make pictures only, with no audio at all. Check that you chose one that produces
sound, and I can point you at the screen where that choice is made.

## Q: My video has been rendering forever.
Video is slow by nature and minutes are normal. If it has plainly stopped rather than slowed, let me
know and I will get the problem written up.

## Q: The video does not match what I described.
Video models drift more than picture models, especially on long clips. Shorter clips with one clear
action work far better, and I can log it if a simple request keeps coming out wrong.

## Q: I cannot sign in.
Check the email address you are using first, since a small typo is the most common cause. If it looks
right and still fails, tell me and I will get it in front of Fred today.

## Q: My sign-in code never arrived.
It can take a couple of minutes, and it does sometimes land in your spam folder. Look there first,
then ask for a fresh code.

## Q: I am locked out of my account.
Repeated failed payments will lock an account to stop a debt piling up quietly. Fixing the payment
method clears it, and if that is not the cause I will escalate it for you.

## Q: It says my access code is not valid.
Access codes are single use, so a code already redeemed will be refused. Check whether you or someone
else has used it, and I will pass it on if you are certain it is fresh.

## Q: It keeps signing me out.
That usually means the sign-in on this device has expired and needs renewing. Sign in once more, and
if it throws you out again the same day I will file it.

## Q: I think I was charged wrong.
Charges are worked out exactly, down to fractions of a credit, so a genuine error is worth
investigating properly. Tell me what you expected and what you saw, and I will record it for Fred
with the figures.

## Q: My credits are missing.
Let me check the obvious causes first: credits attached to an invite are held until your first real
purchase, and a long job can consume more than it looks. If the numbers still do not add up I will
escalate it.

## Q: My payment was declined.
The decline comes from your bank or card provider, so they are the only ones who can tell you why.
Once you have a working card, tell me a dollar amount and I will take you to the secure payment page
to try again.

## Q: I want a refund.
Failed pictures and failed video refund themselves automatically, so check your balance first.
Anything beyond that is Fred's call, and I will put the request in front of him with your reasons.

## Q: I spent far more than I expected.
Let us stop the bleeding first, then work out where it went. I can set your spending ceiling lower
and turn the automatic top-up off if you confirm it, and heavy models are usually the culprit.

## Q: My connected service has stopped working.
Connections to outside services expire after a while and go quiet without complaining. Reconnecting
it is almost always the whole fix.

## Q: It says I need to reconnect something. What does that mean?
The permission you gave that other company has run out, so the app can no longer act on your behalf
there. Signing in to that service again restores it.

## Q: Can you reconnect it for me?
No, and this is the one job I genuinely cannot do. Reconnecting needs your own password on the other
company's own site, so I will take you to the right screen and wait.

## Q: The buttons on my phone do nothing when I tap them.
Reload the page first, since a half-loaded screen looks alive and is not. If the same button is still
dead after a reload, tell me which one and I will report it.

## Q: I cannot find the menu.
Tell me what you are trying to reach and I will take you straight there. The layout moves depending
on how wide your screen is, which is why menus hide on smaller ones.

## Q: The text is cut off at the edge of my screen.
Turning the phone sideways usually recovers the missing part. If something is still clipped, describe
what you see and I will log it as a layout fault.

## Q: The app is very slow to move around.
A long conversation or a big list of saved work will drag on a phone. Starting a fresh conversation
usually restores the speed, and I will record it if a plain empty screen is slow too.

## Q: The screen is blank.
Reload it once, since a page that failed to finish loading shows nothing at all. If it is still empty
after a reload, tell me which screen and I will get it in front of Fred.

## Q: My chat is missing on my other device.
Your conversations live with your account, so signing in with the same email on both should show the
same list. If one device is short of a conversation, tell me its name and I will look for it.

## Q: I closed the tab by accident and lost my conversation.
It is almost certainly still saved. Your past conversations stay in the archive in the sidebar, and
there is a search box there if you remember a phrase from it.

## Q: If the app loses something of mine, can it be recovered?
Usually yes. Records are backed up every night in encrypted form, and built files sit in your own
folder on your own machine, so there are two places to recover from.

## Q: How do I report a problem?
Tell me what happened in your own words and I will write it up as a proper ticket. I will ask whether
you want to be contacted about it before I file anything.

## Q: How do I reach a human?
Through me. I put your problem in front of Fred, who built this app, and he is the person who answers
support issues.

## Q: How long until someone answers me?
Fred is one person, so it depends on how bad the problem is and what else is burning. Anything that
stops you working outright goes to the top of the list.

## Q: What happens after I report something?
It becomes a real ticket with your description attached, and it goes to Fred. Nothing about it is
thrown away or quietly closed without an answer.

## Q: Will you tell me when it is fixed?
Yes, if you say you want to hear back. I can follow up with you once the problem has been dealt with
so you are not left guessing, and you can ask me where a report stands at any time.
