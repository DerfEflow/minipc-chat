## Ask-vs-assume rule (reversibility × cost of being wrong)
- Reversible + cheap → assume, record, keep moving.
- Reversible + expensive → assume, record, verify before building on it.
- Irreversible + cheap → assume with a note.
- Irreversible + expensive → STOP AND ASK. The only quadrant that interrupts the user.
